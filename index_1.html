Praveen Jeevanantham is an Indian embedded hardware engineer and research scientist based in Chennai, Tamil Nadu. He works at SAMEER, a research and development centre under the Department of Information Technology, Government of India, where his work centres on embedded hardware design, firmware development, and embedded Linux systems. He has hands-on experience with STM32 microcontrollers, FMCW mmWave radar configuration, PCB design and bring-up, and serial communication protocols including SPI, UART, and I2C.

Early life and education

Jeevanantham completed his secondary schooling at Kendriya Vidyalaya CLRI in 2017, followed by higher secondary studies at Anna Gem SPS in 2019. He went on to earn a Bachelor of Engineering in Electronics and Communication Engineering from KCG College of Technology in 2023, graduating with a CGPA of 7.08.

Career

Research Scientist at SAMEER (2024–present)

Since 2024, Jeevanantham has worked as a Research Scientist in the Embedded Hardware and Control Systems group at SAMEER. He has contributed to the hardware architecture, component selection, and schematic-level troubleshooting for a Fire Control System, and led PCB design and bring-up of power supply subsystems for an Integrated Control System, focusing on stable DC rail outputs and noise filtering under operational loads. He developed serial communication firmware implementing SPI and UART protocols for reliable data transmission between embedded subsystems, and has carried out hardware debugging and root-cause analysis on PCB assemblies using oscilloscopes and multimeters.

He has also worked extensively with the Venus single-board computer running a custom Linux operating system, flashing custom OS images, configuring device drivers, and interfacing with GPIO and DIO through Linux sysfs and kernel interfaces. He writes shell scripts for automated hardware control and peripheral testing, and debugs hardware-software interaction issues by analyzing kernel logs and driver initialization sequences.

Intern Railway Scientist at Lab to Market Innovations (2022)

In 2022, Jeevanantham interned as a Railway Scientist at Lab to Market Innovations Pvt Ltd, based at the CSIR Taramani campus, where he worked on the application of sensors in fiber optic communication. He developed Fiber Bragg Grating (FBG)-based structural health monitoring systems for railway infrastructure, including sensor calibration, data acquisition, and signal analysis to detect structural strain and deformation.

Notable projects

Custom SPI clock signal generation

Using an STM32F303E microcontroller, Jeevanantham designed bare-metal firmware to generate a precise 30-cycle SPI clock signal by directly configuring GPIO and SPI peripheral registers without HAL abstraction. He validated waveform accuracy and timing with an oscilloscope and optimized the register-level configuration for minimal latency.

Custom DC power supply PCB design

He designed a noise-free, regulated DC power supply circuit in Siemens PADS, carrying out the full PCB layout: schematic capture, component placement, trace routing, and Gerber file generation. The design included DC filtering stages to suppress ripple, with a review for EMI compliance and thermal management.

Smart metering plug (IoT power monitor)

Jeevanantham built an affordable IoT-based smart metering plug to monitor real-time AC power consumption and the battery state-of-charge of connected electric vehicles, integrating current and voltage sensors with an Arduino microcontroller and implementing firmware for data acquisition and serial output.

E-commerce website

He also built a responsive e-commerce frontend using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, featuring client-side form validation, interactive UI components, and a consistent design system.
